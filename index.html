OVERDUE!

Night Shift at the Meme Library

The library is closed. The books are not. Keep the peace by any (quiet) means necessary.

On a phone: drag anywhere to move — books aim and throw themselves. Tap DASH and SHHH with your other thumb.

  • Dust Bunny hops after you.
  • Overdue Notice fast, zig-zagging paperwork.
  • Loud Patron keeps away and shouts sound rings — dash through them.
  • Bookworm a chain; shoot the head and the next segment takes over.
  • Late Fee Golem tanky, bursts into coins.
  • Rogue Author every 5th wave — a boss named after one of the library's authors.
  • ★ Elites (story) wear a gold crown, take more hits and fire pages at you. Their halo flashes red just before they shoot.

Chain kills for a combo multiplier (up to ×4). Clear a wave without getting hit for a flawless bonus. Shelves block enemy pages — use them as cover.
