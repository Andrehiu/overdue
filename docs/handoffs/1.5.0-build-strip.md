## Changes (keep the scope to exactly these)
1. **Short names.** Add a `short` field to every entry in `UPGRADES` (src/upgrades.js): ≤ 13 characters, recognisable
   (e.g. Dewey, Trilogy, Hardcover, Speed Reader, Spine Breaker, Nook, Loan, Espresso, Grace Period, Footnotes, Audiobook,
   Quiet Please, Bookmark, Plot Twist, Extended Loan; combos/masterworks/curses too). Pick sensible ones; a test enforces the length.
2. **Pure helper** in src/upgrades.js: `buildChips(owned, offered = [])` → one entry per owned upgrade, sorted like today
   (master, combo, perk, cursed): `{ id, icon, short, name, kind, lvl, max, linked }`, where `linked` is true when the
   upgrade is a perk that forms a not-yet-owned combo with any card in `offered` (use `comboPartners`).
3. **Build strip as chips.** `renderBuild()` renders each entry as a chip: icon + short name + pips (perks) or a small kind
   label (combo / master / cursed). Chips wrap; on iPhone landscape the strip may take at most 2 rows and the perk screen must
   still fit without scrolling (existing QA "4 cards fit on screen" must keep passing). Use the existing kind colours.
4. **Visual link.** On the perk screen, chips with `linked: true` glow (subtle pulse, respects prefers-reduced-motion) and show 🔗.
   Card hints include the partner's icon: "🔗 Completes a combo! + your 🧭 Dewey → 🗺️ Choose Your Own Adventure" and
   "🔗 Combos with 🧭 Dewey or 📕 Hardcover" (use `short` names in hints too).
5. **Tap a chip for details** (perk screen and pause screen): a small popover with icon + full name, what it does in plain player
   words (`desc`, never internal units: CLAUDE.md "Perk cards speak plainly"), level `●●○ 2/3` (+ "Unlocks <Masterwork> at 3/3"
   for perks below max), and its combos: each as "<combo icon> <combo name>: <partner icon> <partner short> ✓/needed" (✓ if owned,
   or "owned" if the combo is already taken). Closes on tap outside or a second tap. Tapping a chip must never pick a card
   (respect `cardsArmed()` and stop propagation). Keyboard: chips are buttons, Esc closes.
6. CSS in src/game.css, tokens scoped to the component (not `:root`). Text contrast must stay readable (QA checks menu text).
