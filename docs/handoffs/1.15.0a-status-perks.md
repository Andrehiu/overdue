## 1. Shared status engine
- Per-enemy fields for ink, chill and static, ticked inside the existing `updateEnemies` loop. Ink damage lands every 0.5 s
  (merged damage numbers). **Status damage is silent** (no hit sound per tick).
- Chill slows `e.speed * (1 - chill)`; bosses get half the slow.
- **One freeze/stun rule (owner decision):** bosses are immune to freeze and stun from these perks and instead take **+25% damage
  while chilled**. Elites and normal enemies get a **3 s cooldown** after a freeze. Put it in one helper (e.g. `canFreeze(e)`,
  `freeze(e, s)`) so later perks reuse it.
- **Chain generation rule:** damage from ink, zaps or pops never creates more ink, pops or zaps (across frames, not only within
  one frame). Bursts at most 6 per frame; extras are dropped, not queued.
- Per-hit chances scale by `4 / weaponHitsPerSec` (clamped 0.5–2), so fast weapons don't proc more often.
- Visuals: one cached small sprite per status, drawn with `drawImage` beside the enemy; no `shadowBlur`. Zap arcs from a pool of
  at most 12 short polylines (0.12 s). **Colours (owner picked A):** ink violet `#9a6bff` (drop with a light rim `#d9c8ff`),
  chill cyan `#74e6ff` (snowflake + faint body tint), static lime `#c8ff4d` (spark + arc). Add them to `PLAYER` in
  `src/palette.js` so the palette test covers them (none may be an enemy hue).
- Annotated Edition's card says "splash ink": change only its text to "Critical hits burst, hurting everything nearby", so it
  isn't confused with the new ink.
