## Owner decision (defaults approved, owner will test balance after)
After each story room the player chooses the next room from 2 doors. Turns 10 fixed rooms into a route you plan.

| Door | Fight in the next room | Reward for that room |
|---|---|---|
| 💰 Rare Books | elite share +0.5 (eliteChance = min(1, base + 0.5)) | room fines ×2 (`roomReward`) |
| ☕ Break Room | normal fight | heal 1 heart on entering (not above max); room fines ×0.5 rounded up, at least 3 |
| 🎲 Mystery Shelf | normal fight | on clearing: 50% a random cursed book you don't own and haven't banished (applied immediately, with a toast), otherwise (or if none left) a bonus perk pick (an extra perk-card screen) |
| 🚪 The Stacks | normal fight | normal fines. Only offered in place of Break Room when the player is at full health |

Rules:
- Doors are offered after rooms 1–3 and 6–8 (choosing rooms 2–4 and 7–9). After room 4 and room 9 show a single fixed door
  ("★ Elite room" / "Rogue Author") so the flow is the same every room. No door after room 10.
- Two different door types per choice, drawn from Rare Books / Break Room / Mystery Shelf (Break Room → The Stacks at full health).
- Deterministic: offers come from a dedicated seeded RNG (run seed + wing + room), so Renew/Banish/perk rolls never change doors.
- Endless mode: no doors, no changes at all (CLAUDE.md: endless stays as it was).
- **Difficulty only goes up** (CLAUDE.md): a door may make a room harder, never easier. Break Room heals but does not weaken enemies.
- No saved-data changes (doors live only in the current run).
