## Change: a floating aim stick, mirroring the move stick
1. On second-finger `pointerdown`, record the stick **origin** (`touch.ax0/ay0`) at the finger; on move, update `touch.ax/ay`.
   Aim direction = `atan2(ay - ay0, ax - ax0)` (the drag vector), **not** toward the finger's position from the player.
2. **Deadzone:** ~10 CSS px (convert to logical units like the move stick). Inside the deadzone the stick is "not deflected".
   - Auto-aim OFF: throw only while the stick is deflected beyond the deadzone, in the stick's direction. Finger down but not dragged
     = no throwing.
   - Auto-aim ON: a deflected stick overrides auto-aim (manual aim while held); inside the deadzone, auto-aim keeps working.
   - With the Auto-fire setting on and auto-aim off: keep throwing in the last stick direction (as today), but only after the stick
     has been deflected at least once this run.
   Update `aimSource()` (src/util.js) and its unit test accordingly ('touch' only when deflected).
3. **Visual:** draw the aim stick like `drawStick()` (ring 45 + knob 18 at the clamped offset), in the player palette (library blue,
   never red/pink), plus a small arrow or tick on the ring showing the aim direction. Only while the second finger is down.
4. Text: the auto-aim-off hint toast becomes "Auto-aim off: drag a second finger to aim and throw"; the Settings label
   "Auto-aim (off: aim with a second finger)" stays; How to play's throw line becomes "Automatic, aims at the nearest enemy
   (with Auto-aim off in Settings: drag a second finger like a joystick to aim and throw)". Keep the "no keyboard terms" QA check passing.
