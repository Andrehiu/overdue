## Items (owner playtest on iPhone + "Duo" device, all reproduced or read in code by the planner)
1. **Rotate overlay stuck after switching apps (high).** After switching apps and coming back in landscape, the "plays in
   landscape / turn your phone sideways" overlay stays up. Overlay logic: `isPortraitScreen()` (`innerHeight > innerWidth * 1.15`)
   and `updateRotateOverlay()` (`src/main.js` ~59, ~85–96), only re-checked on `resize`. iOS fires a portrait-sized resize while
   the page is hidden/in the app switcher and often no resize on return. Fix: ignore resize-driven overlay/layout changes while
   `document.hidden`; re-check on `visibilitychange` (visible), `pageshow`, `orientationchange` and a
   `matchMedia('(orientation: portrait)')` change listener, plus a delayed re-check (~300 ms). On the title screen also re-run
   `applyLayout()` then. Keep: a run is paused when actually rotated to portrait; no auto-resume.
   QA: new check that simulates hidden → portrait-sized viewport → back to landscape + visible; overlay must end hidden and the
   arena must fill ≥ 95%.
2. **Pinned bottom row (Back bar) doesn't reach the screen edges.** Its dark band floats above the bottom and is narrower than
   the screen, so scrolled list items show below/beside it. Measured: iPad 1180×820 band ends 24 px above the bottom, spans
   103–1077 of 16–1164 px; iPhone 13 gap 6 px. Cause: `position: sticky; bottom: 0` inside the `.screen` padding and
   `width: 100%` of the content box (`src/game.css` ~394 and the safe-area padding ~413). Fix for every screen with the pinned
   row (Codex, Hall of Fame, Renovations, Story, Settings, game over): the band reaches the bottom edge and the full width
   (negative margins equal to the screen padding, safe-area bottom padding inside the band). QA: extend "pinned row covers
   nothing" with "band reaches the bottom edge and spans the width while the list is scrolled".
3. **4–5 perk cards must fit.** With the Renovation that adds a card (`game.cards = 4`, up to 5), the pick screen wraps 3 + 1
   on some devices and pushes the build strip off; iPhone 15 Pro Max (932×430) overflows by 25 px. Fix: 4 and 5 cards always
   one row, cards shrink to fit, no scrolling at any QA size. QA: perk screen with `game.cards = 4` and `5` on every QA device:
   no overflow, build strip visible.
4. **"Added to your Library Codex" toast covers the game-over heading** ("Best shifts on this device" and "Fines banked" row).
   Move or time it so it never overlaps the end-screen headings (e.g. show it below the title area or after the screen settles).
5. **Sound after switching apps.** iOS can leave the AudioContext suspended/"interrupted"; today it is only resumed in
   `sound.init()` at run start (`src/audio.js` ~30). Resume on `visibilitychange` (visible) and on the next user touch.
   Test the logic you can in Playwright (state transitions); say plainly what only a real iPhone can confirm.
6. **CI-only flake:** GitHub's CI (`npm run qa -- --ci`) failed `screens/iphone-se-landscape · room 7 boss spawns` and
   `story boss shows its taunt` on a slow runner (local QA green). Make those checks deterministic (drive time with `stepN`
   / wait on a condition, not wall-clock timeouts). Don't weaken what they check.
