## Owner decision
Some story rooms ask for something other than "kill everything", so rooms play differently. Endless is unchanged.

| Goal | Win condition | Fail state | HUD line |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⏱️ Survive | stay alive for 30 s while enemies keep coming (spawns continue until the timer ends, then the room clears: remaining enemies vanish in a SHHH wave) | you die, as usual | "Survive 0:23" |
| 📚 Rescue | pick up 5 glowing stolen books placed in reachable free cells away from the spawn zone; enemies spawn as normal | you die | "Rescued 2/5" |

## Rules
- Which rooms: deterministic per (wing, room) via a pure `roomGoal(wing, room)` in `src/story.js`; never rooms 1, 5 (elite), 10 (boss);
  at most 2 goal rooms per wing; wings 1–2 at most 1. Doors (v1.7.0) are unaffected: a door's modifiers still apply on top.
- **Difficulty only goes up** (CLAUDE.md): a goal room's total spawn budget is ≥ the plain room's budget (Survive keeps spawning,
  so its budget is the plain budget × time factor; Rescue keeps the plain budget). Add `threat`-based tests proving goal rooms are
  never easier than the plain room they replace, and that the existing every-room/every-wing-harder tests pass.
- Rescue books: placed using the room layout (free cells, reachable per the flow field, ≥ 6 cells from the player start, not in
  the spawn edge ring), deterministic per room. Pickups use the existing pickup drawing style but in the player palette (never red/pink).
- Room clear rewards: same fines as the room would pay (door multipliers still apply).
- Banner subtitle on room start names the goal ("⏱️ Survive 30 seconds", "📚 Rescue 5 stolen books").
