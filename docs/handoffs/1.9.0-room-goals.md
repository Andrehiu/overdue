## Part 2: ⏱️ Survive rooms
- Pure `roomGoal(wing, room)` in `src/story.js` → `'clear'` or `'survive'`, deterministic per (wing, room). Never room 1, the elite

- Survive room: a 30 s timer starts when the room starts; enemies keep spawning for the whole timer (loop/extend the room's spawn
