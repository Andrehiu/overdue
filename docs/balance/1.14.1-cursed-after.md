# 1.14.1 cursed books and Rare Books: after

Changes: Banned Book also pierces 1; Loud Reader SHHH also +50% size and +50% stun time; Lights Out +50% fines and score (fines apply in story only). Costs unchanged.
Same bot set-up as the before file (half Renovations, policy `mystery`, 60 runs per cell).

| Wing | Runs | Take cursed: clear | Walk away: clear | Take, fines/run | Walk, fines/run |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 60 | 100% | 100% | 150 | 135 |
| 4 | 60 | 97% | 80% | 264 | 209 |
| 7 | 60 | 38% | 33% | 255 | 210 |
| 7 (extra) | 150 | 41% (62/150) | 38% (57/150) | 247 | 223 |

Before to after, take minus walk: wing 4 +4 pp to +17 pp; wing 7 (150 runs) +8 pp to +3 pp (inside noise). Taking a cursed book is about the clear rate of a normal pick, with higher fines (Lights Out) and a bigger swing. Not conclusive per book: the bot does not separate the three books.

## Rare Books (door line "×2 fines + a better pick")
Rare door (always taken when offered) vs Break Room / The Stacks (always the safe door), half Renovations, 60 runs per cell.

| Wing | Rare, v1.14.0 door + new pick | Rare, softer door (final) | Safe door |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 62% | 58% | 52% |
| 5 | 15% | 30% | 33% |
| 6 | 8% | 18% | 18% |
| 7 | 3% | 13% | 13% |

The pick alone did not close the gap (wings 5-7 were 15/8/3% vs 33/18/13%). Rare door difficulty softened: extra elite share +0.5 to +0.35, spawn budget x1.3 to x1.15, wing 4+ elites +50% HP to +30% HP and fire 30% to 15% faster. Fines stay x2. A bot variant that always takes the stamped card scored worse (28/7/3/0%): the stamped card is a combo path, not the strongest card, so it is a choice, not a forced take.
