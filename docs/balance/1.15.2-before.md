## Why the bot clears wing 2 and the owner doesn't
- At 65% Renovations the bot has **zero** wing-2 deaths (102 runs), and the human-like bot too. There is no failure cause to report: nothing dies. Hearts on entry stay at ~7 all wing; net loss is ≤ 0.5 hearts per room (rooms 5-7 worst).
- Hearts are lost almost only to `ring` (a hazard shockwave; 1.3-1.5 hearts/run in wing 2), then enemy pages (`page:fan`, `page:spiral`), then boss charges. Enemy contact barely registers.
- How the bot differs from a person: it never mis-taps or looks away; it steers from exact enemy positions 30 times a second and predicts page paths 0.35 s ahead; it dashes by rule; it never gets distracted reading perk cards; it picks perks and doors by a fixed policy. Slowing its reactions and adding steering error costs hearts (wing 2 ring 1.33 → 1.53, fan pages 0.01 → 0.25) but does not produce a single death.
- Conclusion: the bot (either setting) cannot reproduce the owner's wing-2 difficulty, so it can't tell us how much −5% helps there. Its useful signal is relative: wings 1-3 are far above the 85% line. The felt difficulty is likely about reading and dodging on a phone with a thumb (hearts lost to pages and rings), which no bot setting here models.
