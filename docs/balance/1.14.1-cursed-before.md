# 1.14.1 cursed books: before (v1.14.0 effects)

Bot, half Renovations, policy `mystery` (always the Mystery Shelf when offered), Mystery cursed book **always taken** vs **always walked away** (new in 1.14.1; "walk" = only the normal pick, the control). Same seeds in both columns.
Effects then: Banned Book +100% dmg / -1 heart; Loud Reader SHHH 3x faster / enemies +15% speed; Lights Out +50% score (hidden in story) / half lantern.

| Wing | Runs | Take cursed: clear | Walk away: clear |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 60 | 100% | 100% |
| 4 | 60 | 92% | 88% |
| 7 | 60 | 42% | 32% |
| 7 (extra) | 150 | 43% (64/150) | 35% (52/150) |

Read: with ±6 pp noise at 60 runs (±4 at 150), taking a cursed book was already about as good as the normal pick, slightly better at wing 7. Lights Out gave no story benefit.
Command: `npm run balance -- --wings=1,4,7 --renos=half --policies=mystery --cursed=take|walk --runs=60`
